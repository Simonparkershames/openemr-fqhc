Accessibility

Every foreground/background pairing the components actually use, measured against WCAG 2.1 from the token values in tokens.css. AA is the bar: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (18pt+, or 14pt bold). A pairing that fails here is a bug in the palette.

Both themes are measured, each from the values its own cascade produces — not just whichever one you happen to be looking at. The dark palette was chosen against a dark ground, and the semantic *-soft tints are where AA is most easily lost, so neither theme is assumed to inherit the other’s result.
